## Description (long version, for sites without a char limit)

Kodwai scores how well you collaborate with AI coding agents like Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex on real coding challenges. Pick a ticket-sized problem, solve it in your own terminal and editor with your own agent, and submit. The CLI captures your code, git history, test runs, agent transcript, and time, then scores the session across three axes: Direction (how you steer, verify, decompose, and recover), Outcome (what actually shipped and whether it passes), and Lift (the edge cases a one-shot prompt misses). Every signal cites its own evidence, so you can see exactly why each axis scored the way it did.

Climb a public leaderboard, earn badges, filter by agent, and build a profile that shows how you engineer, not what you memorized. Free for developers, bring your own agent. Companies hiring in the agent era can run AI-native interviews on the same engine: candidates solve a real ticket on their own machine, you watch the session live, and it arrives scored against your own rubric.
