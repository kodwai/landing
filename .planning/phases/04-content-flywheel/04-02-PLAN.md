<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Create outreach message templates</name>
  <files>marketing/outreach/templates.md</files>
  <action>
  Create message templates for each outreach type:

  **Twitter/X DM (influencer):**
  Short, specific, no ask upfront. "Hey [name], saw your thread about [specific topic]. Building something in that space — [one-line pitch]. Would love your take if you have 2 min: [link]"

  **Email (podcast/newsletter):**
  Subject: "[Podcast name] episode idea: Why AI-assisted interviews are fundamentally broken"
  Body: Brief intro, why their audience would care, specific angle for an episode/feature, credentials/context.

  **LinkedIn message (engineering leader):**
  Professional but not stiff. Reference their company's approach to AI in hiring. Ask for feedback, not promotion.

  **Community post (Discord/Slack):**
  Value-first, not promotional. Share an insight, contribute to discussion, mention product only if directly relevant.

  **Guest post pitch (blog):**
  Propose a specific article title and outline. Offer genuine value to their audience. Mention waitlist subtly.

  Each template should have:
  - The template with [PLACEHOLDER] fields
  - A filled-in example for one specific target
  - Tips for personalizing
  </action>
  <verify>All 5 template types created with examples</verify>
