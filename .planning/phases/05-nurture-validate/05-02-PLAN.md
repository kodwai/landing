<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Design the validation survey</name>
  <files>marketing/validation/survey-design.md</files>
  <action>
  Create a survey designed to validate (or invalidate) the business idea. Max 10 questions, takes <3 minutes.

  **Section 1: Who are you? (2 questions)**
  Q1: What's your role? [Engineering Manager / VP/Director of Engineering / Recruiter/TA / Software Engineer / Other]
  Q2: Company size? [1-50 / 51-200 / 201-1000 / 1000+]

  **Section 2: Current state (3 questions)**
  Q3: Do you currently use a technical assessment platform? [Yes, which one: ___ / No / Evaluating options]
  Q4: Does your company allow candidates to use AI tools during interviews? [Yes / No / Considering it / Don't know]
  Q5: What's your biggest frustration with current technical interviews? [Open text, max 200 chars]

  **Section 3: AI Interview interest (3 questions)**
  Q6: How valuable would it be to see a full transcript of how a candidate uses AI during a coding challenge? [1-5 scale: Not valuable → Extremely valuable]
  Q7: Which feature matters most to you? [Rank 1-3]
    - Native Claude Code integration (real AI agent, not a chatbot)
    - Full session transcript with AI interaction replay
    - AI-powered candidate scoring and evaluation
    - Standardized AI-skill assessment framework
    - Custom challenge creation for your team

  **Section 4: Willingness to pay (2 questions)**
  Q8: (Van Westendorp) At what price per interview would this be... [4 sub-questions]
    - So cheap you'd question the quality? [$___]
    - A great deal? [$___]
    - Getting expensive but still worth it? [$___]
    - Too expensive to consider? [$___]
  Q9: Would you sign up for a pilot program if we launched in the next 3 months? [Yes, definitely / Probably / Unlikely / No]

  **Closing:**
  Q10: Anything else you'd want us to know? [Open text]
  + "Want early access when we launch? Leave your email." (capture upgrade from waitlist to pilot interest)

  **Implementation:** Use Tally.so (free, no-code, great UX) or Typeform (free tier). Include the direct link and an embeddable version for the site.
  </action>
  <verify>Survey is 10 questions, estimated <3 minutes, covers all validation signals</verify>
  <done>Complete survey design ready to build in Tally/Typeform</done>
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Create analysis framework</name>
  <files>marketing/validation/analysis-framework.md</files>
  <action>
  Create a framework for interpreting survey results:

  **Green light signals (build it):**
  - >60% of respondents rate transcript value at 4-5
  - >40% say "Yes, definitely" to pilot program
  - Van Westendorp sweet spot is >$30/interview
  - >30% of respondents are engineering managers or directors
  - Open text responses reveal specific pain points we solve

  **Yellow light signals (iterate positioning):**
  - 40-60% rate transcript value at 4-5
  - 20-40% say "Yes, definitely" to pilot
  - Sweet spot is $15-30/interview
  - Most respondents are engineers (not buyers)

  **Red light signals (pivot or kill):**
  - <40% rate transcript value at 4-5
  - <20% say "Yes, definitely" to pilot
  - Sweet spot is <$15/interview
  - Open text suggests different problem than what we're solving

  **Metrics dashboard (track weekly):**
  - Waitlist size (raw count + growth rate)
  - Waitlist → survey completion rate (target: >15%)
  - Email open rates (target: >40%)
  - Email reply rate (target: >5%)
  - Survey response distribution by role
  - WTP sweet spot calculation
  - Pilot interest rate

  **Decision framework:**
  At 200 survey responses OR 60 days (whichever comes first):
  - All green → start building the product
  - Mixed → talk to 10 respondents 1:1 for qualitative depth
  - All red → pivot the positioning or kill the idea (save the engineering effort)
  </action>
  <verify>Framework has clear thresholds for go/no-go, all metrics defined</verify>
  <done>Analysis framework ready to apply once survey responses come in</done>
</task>
