<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Create launch day playbook</name>
  <files>marketing/LAUNCH-PLAYBOOK.md</files>
  <action>
  Create a detailed, time-sequenced launch playbook:

  **Pre-Launch (Day Before):**
  - Schedule PH listing for 12:01 AM PT
  - Pre-write all social posts with links
  - Email waitlist teaser: "Something big dropping tomorrow"
  - Test all links, forms, analytics

  **Launch Day (Day 0) Timeline:**

  12:01 AM PT — Product Hunt goes live
  - Post maker comment immediately
  - Share PH link in relevant communities

  7:00 AM PT — Morning wave (US East Coast waking up)
  - Post Twitter/X launch thread
  - Post LinkedIn announcement (Post 1)
  - Submit Show HN

  9:00 AM PT — Reddit wave
  - Post r/cscareerquestions
  - Post r/ExperiencedDevs

  12:00 PM PT — Midday engagement
  - Reply to all PH comments
  - Engage with Twitter replies
  - Post standalone tweet #1
  - Reply to HN comments

  3:00 PM PT — Afternoon push
  - Post r/recruiting
  - LinkedIn Post 2 (thought leadership)
  - Standalone tweet #2

  6:00 PM PT — Evening wrap
  - Twitter engagement (reply, retweet supporters)
  - PH comment replies
  - Reddit engagement

  **Day 1 (Post-Launch):**
  - Morning: Standalone tweet #3 + engagement
  - Midday: Cross-reference best PH comments/reviews in tweets
  - Afternoon: Standalone tweet #4
  - Share any press/coverage

  **Day 2-7 (Sustain):**
  - Daily standalone tweet
  - Respond to all comments across platforms
  - Share user testimonials/reactions
  - Second LinkedIn post if first performed well

  **Engagement Rules:**
  - Reply to EVERY comment on PH within 1 hour
  - Reply to EVERY comment on HN within 2 hours
  - Be authentic, not salesy, in all replies
  - If someone raises a valid concern, acknowledge it genuinely
  - Cross-link: PH comments → tweet about them, HN discussion → LinkedIn mention
  </action>
  <verify>Playbook covers all channels with specific times and actions</verify>
  <done>Complete launch playbook ready to execute</done>
