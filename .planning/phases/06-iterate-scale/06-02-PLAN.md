<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Create go/no-go decision document</name>
  <files>marketing/validation/go-no-go.md</files>
  <action>
  Create the decision framework:

  **Decision Date:** 8 weeks after launch (or 200 survey responses, whichever first)

  **Scorecard (weight each signal):**

  | Signal | Green (3 pts) | Yellow (1 pt) | Red (0 pts) | Weight |
  |--------|---------------|---------------|-------------|--------|
  | Waitlist size | >2,000 | 500-2,000 | <500 | 2x |
  | Pilot interest | >40% "definitely" | 20-40% | <20% | 3x |
  | WTP sweet spot | >$30/interview | $15-30 | <$15 | 3x |
  | Transcript value | >60% rate 4-5 | 40-60% | <40% | 2x |
  | Buyer ratio | >30% managers | 15-30% | <15% | 2x |
  | Inbound inquiries | >20 companies | 5-20 | <5 | 1x |
  | Organic growth | Growing WoW | Flat | Declining | 1x |

  **Scoring:**
  - 30+ points → GO: Start building MVP immediately
  - 20-29 points → CONDITIONAL: Talk to 10 respondents, refine positioning, extend waitlist by 4 weeks
  - <20 points → NO-GO: Pivot positioning dramatically or archive the project

  **If GO — immediate next steps:**
  1. Email pilot-interested respondents with timeline
  2. Create product BRIEF.md and ROADMAP.md
  3. Start building MVP (Claude Code integration, session capture, basic scoring)
  4. Target: MVP in hands of 5 pilot companies within 8 weeks of GO decision

  **If CONDITIONAL — next steps:**
  1. Schedule 10 video calls with survey respondents
  2. Identify the gap between their needs and our positioning
  3. Revise messaging and re-survey
  4. Set new decision date 4 weeks out

  **If NO-GO — next steps:**
  1. Send honest email to waitlist: "We're rethinking our approach based on your feedback"
  2. Document learnings
  3. Decide: pivot (different angle on same market) or archive
  </action>
  <verify>Scorecard is quantitative, all outcomes have clear next steps</verify>
  <done>Complete go/no-go framework ready for week-8 decision</done>
</task>
