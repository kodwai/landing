<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Create KPI dashboard spec</name>
  <files>marketing/analytics/kpi-dashboard.md</files>
  <action>
  Define the KPIs and tracking setup:

  **North Star Metric:** Waitlist signups (weekly)

  **Leading Indicators:**
  - Website traffic (daily)
  - Social media impressions (daily)
  - Email open rates (per send)
  - Survey completion rate (weekly)

  **Lagging Indicators:**
  - Waitlist total (cumulative)
  - Pilot interest rate (from survey Q9)
  - WTP sweet spot (from survey Q8)
  - Inbound inquiries from companies (count)

  **Channel-Specific KPIs:**
  | Channel | KPI | Target (Week 1) | Target (Week 4) | Target (Week 8) |
  |---------|-----|------------------|------------------|------------------|
  | Landing page | Conversion rate | 15% | 20% | 25% |
  | Twitter/X | Impressions/week | 10K | 25K | 50K |
  | LinkedIn | Post engagement | 3% | 5% | 5% |
  | Product Hunt | Upvotes | 100 | - | - |
  | HN | Points | 50 | - | - |
  | Email | Open rate | 50% | 45% | 40% |
  | Survey | Completion rate | 10% | 15% | 20% |

  **Milestones:**
  - Week 2: 100 waitlist signups
  - Week 4: 500 waitlist signups + PH launch complete
  - Week 6: 1,000 waitlist signups + 50 survey responses
  - Week 8: 2,000 waitlist signups + go/no-go decision

  **Tools:**
  - Plausible/Vercel Analytics for traffic
  - Email platform dashboard for email metrics
  - Twitter Analytics for social
  - Tally/Typeform for survey responses
  - Simple spreadsheet for weekly roll-up (or build a /dashboard page on the site)
  </action>
  <verify>All KPIs defined with targets, tools identified</verify>
  <done>KPI framework ready for tracking from day 1</done>
