<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Create brand voice guidelines</name>
  <files>marketing/VOICE.md</files>
  <action>
  Short doc defining the brand voice:

  **Tone:** Confident, technical, slightly irreverent. We're engineers talking to engineers. No corporate HR-speak. No "leverage" or "synergize" or "empower."

  **Voice principles:**
  - Direct over diplomatic ("This is broken" not "There's an opportunity to improve")
  - Specific over vague ("Claude Code runs terminal commands" not "AI-powered coding assistance")
  - Opinionated over neutral ("Chat-based AI in interviews is a joke" not "There may be limitations to current approaches")
  - Brief over verbose (if you can cut a word, cut it)

  **Words we use:** agent, ship, build, engineer, evaluate, transcript, session, real
  **Words we avoid:** leverage, empower, synergize, solution, robust, scalable (unless literally about scaling), seamless

  **Format conventions:**
  - "Claude Code" always capitalized, always two words
  - "AI Interview" is the product name
  - Use em-dashes for asides — not parentheses
  - Numbers under 10 spelled out, 10+ as digits
  </action>
