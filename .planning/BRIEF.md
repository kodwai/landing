# AI Interview - Waitlist Launch & Idea Validation

**One-liner**: Launch a waitlist landing page and marketing campaign to validate demand for a technical interview platform with native Claude Code integration.

## Problem

Every major tech company now allows AI in interviews, but all existing platforms (CodeSignal, CoderPad, HackerRank, Codility) offer dumbed-down chat assistants — not real AI coding agents. No platform lets candidates use Claude Code natively, with full session capture, transcripts, and AI-generated scoring. The market needs a purpose-built platform that evaluates how engineers actually work with AI agents, not chatbots.

## Success Criteria

How we know it worked:

- [ ] Landing page live and collecting emails within 1 week
- [ ] 500+ waitlist signups within first 30 days
- [ ] 2,000+ waitlist signups within 60 days
- [ ] At least 20 inbound inquiries from engineering managers/recruiters
- [ ] Social media content generates 50K+ impressions in first month
- [ ] Validated willingness-to-pay via survey responses from waitlist

## Constraints

- Bootstrapped / zero ad budget — organic growth only
- Solo founder — all execution via Claude + automation
- No product to demo yet — waitlist + vision only
- Must move fast — competitors are entering the space weekly

## Out of Scope

- Building the actual interview platform (that's post-validation)
- Paid advertising or sponsored content
- Enterprise sales outreach
- Building a blog CMS — use static pages or simple tools
