# Roadmap: AI Interview - Waitlist Launch

## Overview

Go from zero to a validated idea with thousands of waitlist signups in 8 weeks. Build a high-converting landing page, launch across every relevant channel, create viral content, nurture signups, and measure demand signals — all organically, all bootstrapped.

## Phases

- [ ] **Phase 1: Landing Page & Waitlist Infrastructure** - Build the landing page, email capture, and analytics
- [ ] **Phase 2: Content Arsenal** - Create all launch content (copy, threads, posts, visuals)
- [ ] **Phase 3: Launch Blitz** - Simultaneous launch across HN, Product Hunt, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Reddit
- [ ] **Phase 4: Content Flywheel & Community** - Ongoing content, community engagement, partnerships
- [ ] **Phase 5: Nurture & Validate** - Email sequences, surveys, demand signal analysis
- [ ] **Phase 6: Iterate & Scale** - Double down on what works, optimize conversion, plan product

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Landing Page & Waitlist Infrastructure
**Goal**: Live landing page collecting emails with analytics tracking
**Depends on**: Nothing (first phase)
**Plans**: 3 plans

Plans:
- [ ] 01-01: Project setup (Next.js/Astro, Tailwind, deployment to Vercel)
- [ ] 01-02: Landing page design and build (hero, value props, social proof, waitlist form)
- [ ] 01-03: Email capture backend (Resend/Loops), analytics (Plausible/PostHog), OG images

### Phase 2: Content Arsenal
**Goal**: Ready-to-publish content for every channel before launch
**Depends on**: Phase 1 (need landing page URL)
**Plans**: 3 plans

Plans:
- [ ] 02-01: Core messaging doc — positioning, taglines, key narratives, competitive angles
- [ ] 02-02: Social content — Twitter/X thread, LinkedIn post, Reddit posts (r/cscareerquestions, r/ExperiencedDevs, r/recruiting), HN Show HN post
- [ ] 02-03: Long-form content — "Why AI-assisted interviews are broken" blog post, Meta interview analysis piece

### Phase 3: Launch Blitz
**Goal**: Coordinated multi-channel launch hitting all platforms within 48 hours
**Depends on**: Phase 2
**Plans**: 2 plans

Plans:
- [ ] 03-01: Product Hunt launch prep (listing, assets, maker comment, hunter outreach)
- [ ] 03-02: Launch execution plan — posting schedule, engagement strategy, cross-linking

### Phase 4: Content Flywheel & Community
**Goal**: Sustain momentum with ongoing content and community engagement
**Depends on**: Phase 3
**Plans**: 3 plans

Plans:
- [ ] 04-01: Weekly content calendar (4 weeks of Twitter/X threads, LinkedIn posts, Reddit engagement)
- [ ] 04-02: Influencer/partnership outreach list — dev Twitter, engineering leaders, recruiting influencers, podcast guests
- [ ] 04-03: Community plays — commenting strategy, cross-promotions, engineering Discord/Slack communities

### Phase 5: Nurture & Validate
**Goal**: Convert waitlist signups into validated demand signals
**Depends on**: Phase 1 (email infra)
**Plans**: 2 plans

Plans:
- [ ] 05-01: Email welcome sequence (3-email drip: welcome + vision, problem deep-dive, survey/feedback ask)
- [ ] 05-02: Validation survey design — willingness to pay, feature priorities, interview format preferences

### Phase 6: Iterate & Scale
**Goal**: Analyze results, double down on winning channels, plan product buildout
**Depends on**: Phases 3-5
**Plans**: 2 plans

Plans:
- [ ] 06-01: Analytics review — channel attribution, conversion rates, engagement metrics, survey results
- [ ] 06-02: Go/no-go decision framework and product roadmap outline based on validation data
